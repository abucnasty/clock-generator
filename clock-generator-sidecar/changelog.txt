Version: 0.12.0
  Features:
    - exports cars, tanks, cargo wagons and rocket silos as chests: a wagon's quality, the slot filters of a car, tank or wagon, and a silo's recipe quality, crafting speed and productivity. They are exported empty, whatever they hold when selected
    - exports a mining drill that drops into a chest, a car, a tank, a cargo wagon or a rocket silo
    - exports each mining drill's productivity, its research and productivity modules together, in place of one mining productivity level for every drill
  Bugfixes:
    - a mining drill that drops into something not in the JSON is listed as left out; earlier versions exported it as dropping into the first machine. One that has not dropped into its car or tank yet is listed too: let it run and select it again
    - the selection tool selects linked belts; earlier versions skipped them, though the sidecar reads them as pieces of a belt
    - an inserter that drops onto a belt or into a chest not in the JSON (empty, or outside the selection) is listed as left out; earlier versions exported it as dropping onto the first belt or into the first chest

Version: 0.11.0
Date: 2026-10-21
  Features:
    - captures pickups over the hood of an underground exit: lane 1 unmirrored, lane 2 mirrored, half-tile reach
    - captures the pickup layout (side, front or over the hood) apart from the reach and the supply; older underground captures need recapture
    - exports each pickup's classification and source, so a pickup the Clock Generator does not support stays marked through saves and imports
    - captures the preferred lane and half-tile reach in front of an underground entrance: lane 2 mirrored, lane 1 otherwise
  Changes:
    - a pickup from a belt tile that another belt side-loads onto is marked unsupported, as a splitter pickup is; earlier versions exported it as an ordinary belt pickup
  Bugfixes:
    - exports each inserter's kind and quality, and its filter mode for a blacklist; filters switched off are no longer exported. Earlier versions made the Clock Generator run every inserter as a legendary stack inserter

Version: 0.6.0
Date: 2026-10-05
  Features:
    - biochambers are exported with the type "biochamber" and their energy consumption bonus (energy_consumption_bonus, in percent) so the clock generator can work out how many nutrients they burn
    - the version of the mod is exported as sidecar_version so the clock generator can ask for an update when it is older than the version that exports a setting it can use
    - an inserter that picks up from a straight piece of belt is exported with the lane nearest to it (near_lane on its source: 1 for the left lane in the direction the belt runs, 2 for the right one), which is the lane it takes from first when its machine takes the items of both
  Bugfixes:
    - belt pieces joined to each other, through undergrounds and splitters as well, are exported as one belt, with lanes read from all of its pieces. A lane that had run dry under one inserter used to split a belt in two, and an inserter over an empty piece was put on the first belt
    - inserters are exported with only the filters set on the inserter. Filters were guessed from the source machine's recipe or the source belt's lanes, which listed items the sink does not accept and made the config invalid; the clock generator works out what an inserter moves itself
