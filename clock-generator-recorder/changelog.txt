Version: 0.5.0
Date: 2026-10-07
  Features:
    - Remote interface for recording without a player (harness_build, harness_describe, harness_start, harness_status): builds a blueprint string, removes or replaces its clock and wires the new one, seeds items, and records a number of ticks
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.4.0
Date: 2026-10-06
  Features:
    - Record mining drills that drop into a machine: their status, mining progress and bonus mining progress every tick
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-10-06
  Features:
    - Setting "Minimum ticks to record": keeps recording whole clock periods until at least that many ticks are recorded, for a longer look at slow things like fuel
    - Up to 100000 clock periods can be recorded, where 100 was the most
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-10-05
  Features:
    - Record the fuel slot of burner machines (biochambers) every tick, the energy left in the fuel item being burned, and which fuel item is burning
    - Biochambers are exported with the type "biochamber" and their energy consumption bonus (energy_consumption_bonus, in percent)
  Bugfixes:
    - Inserters are exported with only the filters set on the inserter, not filters guessed from the source machine's recipe or the source belt's lanes
    - Starting a recording of a burner machine no longer fails on a Factorio version without the item prototype attribute fuel_category: the fuel items are found with the prototype filter, the attribute, the burner's contents or the biochamber's nutrients, and a burner attribute that is missing while recording records zero instead of an error
