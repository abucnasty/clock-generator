# Game data and what is not modelled yet

This page holds what the generator reads from the game and what it refuses by name; GOALS.md holds the goals.

The generator reads the game's recipes, items, drills, resources, container slots and the rocket silo from a dump of Factorio 2.1.21 (build 87673, `resources/data-version.json`, since 0.14.0; before that an older 2.0 dump that still named the ore melting recipes `molten-iron` and `molten-copper`). Item weights, which the dump names only where a mod sets one, come from the running game of the same install (`resources/dump-item-weights.js`, since 0.20.0). 2.1 gives a recipe a list of crafting categories; the generator reads the list, and the only thing it does with it is tell a biochamber from an assembler in an older sidecar's export. The game's version the data comes from is recorded beside it and printed in the header of `npm run calls`, so a call can be told from one made on other data.

A config describes one copy of a build, and `copies` is the number of identical copies, with no inserter between copies and the belts shared; it may be fractional (5.5) when the build holds a partial block, planned as that fraction of one block's rate and read as an exact fraction, so a whole number or a short decimal (`src/config/schema.ts`). A config that holds several identical copies is refused and told to describe one (`src/config/copies-validator.ts`): the smallest repeating unit is what is planned, never a block of hundreds of inserters.

What the generator does not model is refused by name, with where it is on the roadmap (`docs/ROADMAP.md`), so that a user and the config writer know whether to wait or to work around it:
- A recipe whose only products are fluids (ore melting, acid, oil cracking): follow-up F1, a fluid rate model for the drills and inserters feeding it. Fluid ingredients are dropped from a recipe and the machine is taken to be fed; whether that lets the model's foundry run faster or slower than the game's is a question of milestone M1.
- A rocket silo as a machine, crafting rocket parts and launching: phase 2 of milestone M2. Since 0.20.0 a car, a tank, a cargo wagon standing still and a rocket silo are chests (phase 1): their slots come from the game data (a cargo wagon's by its quality), a storage size on them is refused, and a rocket silo's rocket inventory holds items by their weight and its input slots its rocket part ingredients up to the insertion limit, with no craft run. A car or wagon at the end of a line fills up or runs dry, as a buffer chest does.
- Moving trains (a wagon swapped at a station): not modelled.
- Recyclers and per-quality output: milestone M3. The recycler's recipe data (`shared_probability` per result) is in the dump and read as a result's expected amount (the slice's width); the recycler's output on the tile in front, and its per-quality tiers, are not modelled.
- Inserters other than the legendary stack inserter (since 0.14.0, `prototype` and `quality` in the config): their swing between machines and chests and their hand come from the game data by the rule in [rules-of-the-game.md](rules-of-the-game.md) (measured once); picking up from a belt is refused for every kind and quality but the legendary stack inserter, whose timing is the only one calibrated, and dropping onto a belt keeps its timing (follow-up F7); every kind waits for a full hand as the stack inserter does, although the game data gives only the stack inserter `wait_for_full_hand`. The burner inserter is refused. The sidecar exports both fields from 0.11.0 on and the web form shows them; a capture without them is asked to be recaptured.
- Inserter filters that are a blacklist (`filter_mode: "blacklist"`, exported by the sidecar from 0.11.0 on): refused; `filters` are read as the items to move. Filters switched off in the game are not exported.
- Latches and sensors switching an inserter: not modelled and not compared; such an inserter is one to clock.

What the generator runs without its timing having been checked in the game is generated with a warning instead: a belt pickup from a lane whose `stack_size` is not 4, or with a hand other than 16 (an inserter's `stack_size`, or an item that stacks to less). Belt pickup timing is calibrated with 16-item hands on four-item belt stacks only (`src/config/pickup-calibration.ts`, [experiments](belt-pickup/experiments.md#belt-stack-sizes-and-hand-sizes)); the warning is in the log, among the clocks' insights and in the web form's Build validation, and measuring these is follow-up F7.
