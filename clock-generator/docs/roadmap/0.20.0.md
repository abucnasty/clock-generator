# 0.20.0: containers and real cells

This is the plan for release 0.20.0: what it is for, its packages in order and what it leaves for later. It is kept while the release is in flight and dropped when 0.20.0 is released. Live status is on the Real Boy board, https://github.com/users/abucnasty/projects/2, item "0.20.0 · Containers and real cells"; this page does not follow every merge. The milestones and follow-ups it names are in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).

Nothing here starts before 0.19.0 is released ([0.19.0.md](0.19.0.md)), unless the owner brings containers forward.

## What the release is for

0.19.0 makes belt pickups agree with the game and refuses what it cannot model. 0.20.0 adds containers, then takes on real cells that 0.19.0 can judge only on samples, or only with some inserters left always enabled. Agricultural science is the case in point: it leaves its egg-loop inserters always enabled, it never reaches a proven repeat, and its recovery after a supply loss is judged without spoilage, which the game has.

It advances milestone M2 (containers) first, then milestone M4 (stability on real cells) and milestone M5 (backpressure rule), and follow-up F7 if it is chosen.

The goals keep their order ([GOALS.md](../../GOALS.md)): goal 1, the clock meets its target exactly and recovers; goal 2, the fewest wake-ups. Model corrections are reported apart from search-only changes, and search-only changes keep every result identical. The time spent searching at generation does not grow ([working rules](../working-rules.md), rule 6).

Engine and UI are 0.20.0 and Sidecar the next version (0.12.0 unless the owner sets another), each with one unreleased changelog entry.

## Packages, in order

Packages are named `0.20/<code>`. The numbering follows the board item.

### 1. Containers

**`0.20/containers`** delivers milestone M2: cars and cargo wagons as chests, with wagon slots by quality taken from the game data, and a rocket silo as a chest that holds one rocket by weight, never as a machine. Today these are refused by name ([game-data-and-scope.md](../game-data-and-scope.md)). Done when they generate and are checked on cells farmed from real bases.

### 2. Thaeln's cell 09

**`0.20/thaeln-cell09`**: a plan and a clock that support Thaeln's build (`REAL_THAELN_CELL09`) through the generator's general rules. Its coal inserter moves 15/8 hands a cycle, which stretches every planned clock to eight cycles (1,344 ticks). None of the clocks planned for it is stable, so the generator falls back to the observed-windows search, which takes about ten minutes, by far the slowest of any sample. It starts from a clock Thaeln made for this build that works in the game, which the owner will provide, and turns what its windows do into a planning rule. Done when the generator plans a clock for this build by a rule that applies to any inserter with a fractional hand count, without the observed-windows search; the clock holds in the game with recovery, and every other sample's result is unchanged or checked again in the game.

### 3. Agricultural science: spoiling builds and the egg loop

**`0.20/l2`**, loss recovery for builds whose items spoil. The simulator has no spoilage. In the game a loss longer than an item's spoil time spoils the loop whatever the clock does, so the recovery verdicts of the simulator's long loss trials do not carry over to such builds. Requiring that recovery anyway costs wake-ups: agricultural science selects its 2-swing clock (3,090 wake-ups a minute) over batched swings (2,672) and 4 swings (2,942), which fail only the recovery check. Done when the owner has decided what a stable verdict means for a build whose items spoil (for example, recovery only from losses shorter than the shortest spoil time) and the generator applies it; any selection that changes is checked in the game. It comes before `0.20/l1`, whose recovery check depends on it.

**`0.20/l1`**, clock the egg loop. The inserters that pass eggs between agricultural science's two egg biochambers (5 and 8, 392 wake-ups a minute each) are left always enabled by the current rule for loops ([wake-ups.md](../wake-ups.md)), and so are those that take eggs into the science biochamber (9 and 10, 883 a minute each). Together the four carry 2,550 of the selected clock's 3,090 wake-ups a minute. Done when the inserters in scope get windows that hold the target in the game over at least 63 baseline periods and meet the loss rule `0.20/l2` sets, and the selected clock's wake-ups drop; otherwise the reason is recorded and they stay always enabled.

### 4. Wake-ups and samples

**`0.20/w1`**, the wake-up count against the game. At full output the game counts a held product as finished when it goes into the output, which wakes the output inserter waiting on that machine; the simulator counts it when it is made. On the belted-buffer build that is 20 wake-ups a minute the simulator misses, and the scaffold test carries a temporary allowance (`wake_ups_open`) for it. Counting it the game's way also adds wake-ups in an inserter's own pickup tick on five builds, which may not be real in the game. First settle whether a pickup-tick wake is real, and record the wake-ups of agricultural science, iron bacteria cultivation and the accumulator, which no game recording has yet. Done when the simulator's wake-ups match the game's within the scaffold test's tolerance on every scaffold build with no allowance for this cause, the rule for pickup-tick wakes is decided and documented, and any change in ranking is stated per sample. Goal 2 only: output and the guarantee are not affected.

**`0.20/stupidfathobbit-cell02`**: Stupidfathobbit's cell 02 generates. `REAL_STUPIDFATHOBBIT_CELL02`, a processing-unit cell from the owner's save (#189), is refused in 0.19.0: one inserter puts electronic circuits onto a belt for the rest of a utility science design that the config does not include. Measure that belt's circuit draw from the save, give the lane that export rate, and take the cell off the refusal list. Done when it generates, its result is pinned, and any verdict it gets is checked against its scaffold in the game.

**`0.20/productivity-chain`**: a complete multi-machine chain sample (issue #89) in place of the productivity-module sample 0.19.0 drops, with its inputs, an output belt and a scaffold. Done when the sample generates, is among the samples every engine change is checked against, and its selected clock is checked in the game.

**`0.20/belt-export-time`**: generating the processing-unit sample with a belt export (`PROCESSING_UNITS_BELT_EXPORT`) takes over five minutes, far longer than any other sample (issue #101). Its period is 116,160 ticks, and none of its clocks holds the target. Done when the cause is known and the sample generates in a time in line with the others, or the reason it cannot is recorded on the issue.

### If chosen: other inserters on belts

**`0.20/f7`** delivers follow-up F7. 0.19.0 refuses a belt pickup by anything but the legendary stack inserter and warns about pickups outside 16-item hands on 4-item stacks, because belt timing is calibrated there only; a belt drop by another kind runs on the stack inserter's drop model. Measure in the game, for the kinds and qualities the owner names (by default every kind at normal and legendary quality), pickup and drop timing at all four speeds, moving and backed-up; and for the legendary stack inserter, pickups from 1–3-item stacks and with a smaller hand. Two capture gaps go with it: a non-stack inserter does not stack items on a belt in the game, but the model drops a stacked hand for it; and an inserter with filters on and an empty whitelist is exported like one with filters off. Done when the straight-pickup and belt-drop controls pass for each measured kind, the refusal is lifted for exactly those, and the warning is narrowed to what stays unmeasured.

## Decisions before it starts

- Follow-up F7: whether it is in this release, and which inserter kinds and qualities to calibrate.
- `0.20/l1`: whether inserters 9 and 10 are in scope with the egg loop.
- `0.20/l2`: what a stable verdict means for a build whose items spoil.
- Whether the swing-back hand that 0.19.0 records as a known limit (#276) becomes a model change in this release.

## Not in this release

- **Follow-ups for later:** follow-up F8, the capture contract, and follow-up F9, the Pickup details UI.
- **Out of scope, as in 0.19.0:** splitters as the pickup tile, and pickups from a side-loaded belt tile. A belt fed through a splitter upstream stays modelled as a plain belt unless a real build shows a wrong verdict. Shared withdrawals upstream, sparse or independently phased supply and general conveyor networks stay unsupported. Also not attempted: live inventory or arm snapshots, simulating fluids, power or circuits in general, and rebuilding beacons.
- **Known limits:** those of 0.19.0 stay as they are unless a package above reaches them. The ones in reach: spoilage (`0.20/l2`), the wake-up mechanisms (`0.20/w1`) and belt drops by other inserter kinds (`0.20/f7`).
