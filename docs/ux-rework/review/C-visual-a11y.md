# UX review C: visual design, information design and accessibility

### 1. Gut reaction
The first thing my eye lands on is the orange "Support me on Ko-fi" button. It is the only filled, saturated control on a screen where Generate is a grey disabled box in the corner. The orange and Titillium say Factorio, but the layout is stock MUI dark: seven cards of equal weight stacked on a grey page, every title an h6, and the one hint about what to do first sits below the fold. Results opens with a 140-word paragraph on top of a ten-column table, followed by two charts I can only read by hovering a mouse over canvas pixels. With a keyboard, a screen reader or a phone I get almost nothing, and at 390px wide I cannot even get back to Configure.

### 2. Top 5 changes I'd make tomorrow

1. **Make the header measure its own height (the narrow-width blocker).** At 390px the app bar wraps to about 96px, but the workflow bar is pinned at `top: 56`. The Configure/Results toggle slides under the app bar, and so do Validate/Generate on Configure (30, 39; `WorkflowBar.tsx:75`, `App.tsx:284-306`). The fix: render the workflow bar inside the AppBar as a second dense Toolbar. Below 600px, fold Import/Export into the ⋮ menu and shorten the title so the app bar stays on one line. This is the only place the site cannot be used at all, and the fix is small.

2. **One loud button per state, and a warning that looks like a warning.** Today the loudest control on every screen is Ko-fi, and Generate is grey when a player arrives. On Results, Copy blueprint (the thing the player came for) is outlined, the same weight as Validate (02, 20). The warning color is MUI's default `#ffa726`, a near twin of the brand `#fca300`. So "Changed since validation" and Copy blueprint's below-target state (`WorkflowBar.tsx:107`) look like ordinary orange controls (27, 32). The fix:
   - Fill only the next step in green: Validate, then Generate, then Copy blueprint.
   - Make Ko-fi a link in a footer that does not stick.
   - Give warnings their own yellow (`#ffe14d`, filled, black text, ⚠ icon).
   - In the same pass, move snackbars out from under the footer and the app bar.

   This decides whether a player reaches the blueprint and whether they notice when it is wrong.

3. **Turn Potential Clocks into a decision.** The changes:
   - Replace the 140-word paragraph with one line and a "How clocks are chosen" disclosure, with text capped at 72ch.
   - Put a filled "Recommended: fewest wake-ups for a moving belt" chip on the auto-selected row.
   - Fold constant columns (240.00 ×5, Stable ×5) into a summary line, and add data bars to the wake-ups column.
   - Group unstable rows under a divider.
   - Lead the page with a summary of the selected clock and the Copy button.

   This is the screen where the player chooses, and today it demands reading a manual first.

4. **Make the timelines readable at a glance and without a mouse.** The changes:
   - Stop item colors reusing the state colors.
   - Add a legend for the 6px Factorio-state strip.
   - Make Disabled and Blocked visible (Disabled is about 1.5:1 against the track today).
   - Add gridlines and a "tick" axis.
   - Merge the two timelines into one row per entity.
   - Add a table view and keyboard stepping through transitions.

   These charts are the evidence for "why this clock", and right now most players cannot read them.

5. **An accessibility pass.** The work:
   - Name roughly 20 unnamed controls.
   - Stop tooltips replacing accessible names (`describeChild`).
   - Turn the (i) icons and the other help into focusable click popovers.
   - Add one live status region for validate, generate and copy.
   - Fix the heading outline (no h1; everything is an h6).

   Each item is small. Together they take the site from unusable to usable with a screen reader.

### 4. What I'd cut or hide
- **The Ko-fi button and the sticky footer.** Move Ko-fi to the ⋮ menu or a footer that does not stick. That gives back 46px on every screen and stops the donation link being the loudest control.
- **The Log tab and debug options.** Put them behind "Developer tools" in ⋮.
- **The Potential Clocks paragraph and the "Not searched" alert.** Replace them with a "How clocks are chosen" disclosure.
- **The "Exported clock / Plan" toggle and its paragraph.** Default to the exported clock and move plan-vs-clock into Insights, where the swing comparison already lives.
- **Simplified/Detailed, By Type/By Flow, entity filter and palette filter.** Collapse them into one "View options" menu.
- **The Transfer Activity Timeline as its own card.** Merge it into the state timeline.
- **Constant columns in the clock table.** Rate achieved when every row is equal, Status when every row is Stable.
- **Empty placeholders.** Keep one "+" instead of four empty filter slots. Shrink the empty Chests and the disabled Mining Drills cards to one-line "+ Add" rows.
- **The 3-line Validation explanation.** One line is enough.

### 5. What already works
- State colors were picked on purpose from the Wong colorblind-safe palette (`colors.ts:8-31`), and every legend swatch and verdict chip has a text label. The foundation is right; the problems are reuse and contrast.
- The core palette passes AA where it counts. Orange `#fca300` text is ~7.8:1 on `#232323` and ~5.1:1 on `#404040`. The green buttons use dark text (~8:1) instead of white, which would have been ~2.5:1.
- The fuel timeline (37) answers its question at a glance. It plots fuel level against a dashed limit with the swings beneath, under a plain sentence: "No machine ran out of fuel… The lowest any machine got was 0.76 items". The swing comparison also offers its exact ticks as a table. That is the pattern the other charts should copy.

### 6. Open questions for the author
- Is narrow width a target? Phones may not be, but players likely run the site in a half-width window beside Factorio (720–960px). I only have 1440 and 390 shots; what happens at 800px?
- Who are the timelines for: players checking a clock, or you checking the simulator? If they are for you, Detailed mode, the Plan run, the Factorio strip and Log belong behind a developer toggle.
- Do most players copy the recommended clock without comparing? If so, Results should open with a summary card and Copy, and the table becomes "compare other clocks".
- Should the look follow Factorio's GUI grammar (beveled frames, inset item slots, green confirm buttons, title-bar headers), or stay stock MUI in Factorio colors? The current halfway point is why it reads as generic.
- Was the sticky Ko-fi placement a measured decision? Does it actually convert?
- Are internal item names ("logistic-science-pack") used on purpose to match the JSON and the sidecar, rather than the in-game names?
- Do you know of screen-reader or keyboard-only players? It does not change the obligation, but it would help order the accessibility work.
