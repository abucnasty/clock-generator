# Electromagnetic science with recycler direct insertion

The left repeated cell of the blueprint supplied for [#100](https://github.com/abucnasty/clock-generator/issues/100): two legendary electromagnetic plants making science, one making supercapacitors, and a legendary recycler on processing units. Electronic circuits go directly from the recycler into the supercapacitor plant. Its existing filtered inserter removes advanced circuits into a disposal infinity chest.

Target: **240 science packs/s across five copies**, 48/s for this cell, 24/s for each science plant. This preserves the requested design rate; it is not the maximum. The supplied two-cell blueprint would need 2.5 copies for the same rate. Each plant has five legendary productivity modules: +175% including the plant's built-in bonus. At the target this cell needs 17.4545 supercapacitors/s, 25.3884 electronic circuits/s and 5.07769 processing units/s. The supercapacitor plant's capacity is 17.53125/s, so the target uses 99.5625% of it.

The supplied machines, modules, beacons and retained layouts are unchanged. Two boundary substitutions were approved before use: the holmium plate/superconductor pickup on a bend is supplied from an infinity chest at the same pickup position, and the advanced circuit outlet has a disposal infinity chest. The remaining external supplies and fluid pipes are retained. Belt feeders belong to the scaffold environment and are omitted from the config, as in the harness's boundary convention; their belts are supplied continuously. Sidecar exported the actual speeds, productivity, qualities and pickup geometry after building the cell in Factorio 2.1.21; no unsupported pickup is omitted from the config.

The science plants empty freely into their existing steel chests. The two inserters emptying those chests set the rate: one hand of 16 each every 40 ticks. The other ten inserters stay enabled, including the advanced circuit remover, so the cell keeps all its intermediate buffers available. `terminal_swing_count: 1` asks for one output hand a cycle; `lcm: 1` keeps that cycle at 40 ticks, since the fractional hands of always-enabled intermediate inserters need not repeat on the output clock. This is a working clock for the whole cell, with one decider, rather than a claim that every intermediate inserter has been optimized. The recycler fills its target to the mining drill's stop through the shared placement code.

`pink-science-recycler.json` is the config; `pink-science-recycler-scaffold.txt` builds the cell. `tests/game/builds/recycler-machine-clock.game.test.ts` verifies its generated clock's rate and the target's drill stop in the game.
