# 0.25.0: Gleba

This is the plan for release 0.25.0: what it is for, its packages in order and what it leaves for later. It is kept while the release is in flight and dropped when 0.25.0 is released. Live status is on the Real Boy board, https://github.com/users/abucnasty/projects/2, item "0.25.0 · Gleba"; this page does not follow every merge. The milestones and follow-ups it names are in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).

## What the release is for

Agricultural science is a real cell that 0.19.0 can judge only with some inserters left always enabled: it leaves its egg-loop inserters always enabled, it never reaches a proven repeat, and its recovery after a supply loss is judged without spoilage, which the game has. 0.25.0 first settles what a stable verdict means for a build whose items spoil, then clocks the egg loop.

It advances milestone M4 (stability on real cells).

The goals keep their order ([GOALS.md](../../GOALS.md)): goal 1, the clock meets its target exactly and recovers; goal 2, the fewest wake-ups. Model corrections are reported apart from search-only changes, and search-only changes keep every result identical. The time spent searching at generation does not grow ([working rules](../working-rules.md), rule 6).

Engine and UI are 0.25.0, each with one unreleased changelog entry.

## Packages, in order

Packages are named `0.25/<code>`. The numbering follows the board item.

### 1. Agricultural science: spoiling builds and the egg loop

**`0.25/l2`**, loss recovery for builds whose items spoil. The simulator has no spoilage. In the game a loss longer than an item's spoil time spoils the loop whatever the clock does (an observation, not yet recorded in the game), so the recovery verdicts of the simulator's long loss trials, which run until the build has stopped, do not carry over to such builds. Shorter losses, including the three disturbances recorded in the game, are not affected. Requiring that recovery anyway costs wake-ups: agricultural science selects its 2-swing clock (3,090 wake-ups a minute) over batched swings (2,672) and 4 swings (2,942), which fail only the recovery check. Done when it is decided what a stable verdict means for a build whose items spoil (for example, recovery only from losses shorter than the shortest spoil time) and the generator applies it; any selection that changes is checked in the game. It comes before `0.25/l1`, whose recovery check depends on it.

**`0.25/l1`**, clock the egg loop. The inserters that pass eggs between agricultural science's two egg biochambers (5 and 8, 392 wake-ups a minute each) are left always enabled by the current rule for loops ([wake-ups.md](../wake-ups.md)), and so are those that take eggs into the science biochamber (9 and 10, 883 a minute each). Together the four carry 2,550 of the selected clock's 3,090 wake-ups a minute. Clocking loops can save a lot: on iron bacteria, windows on its two loop inserters held the target in the game for ten minutes at 1,587 wake-ups a minute against 4,490 for the selected clock, and ten minutes on one build was not enough to change the rule. Whether inserters 9 and 10 are in scope is still to be decided (see the decisions below). Done when the inserters in scope get windows that hold the target in the game over at least 63 baseline periods and meet the loss rule `0.25/l2` sets, and the selected clock's wake-ups drop; otherwise the reason is recorded and they stay always enabled.
