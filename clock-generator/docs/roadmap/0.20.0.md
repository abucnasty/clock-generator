# 0.20.0: containers and drills

This is the plan for release 0.20.0: what it is for, its packages in order and what it leaves for later. It is kept while the release is in flight and dropped when 0.20.0 is released. Live status is on the Real Boy board, https://github.com/users/abucnasty/projects/2, item "0.20.0 · Containers and drills"; this page does not follow every merge. The milestones and follow-ups it names are in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).

## What the release is for

0.20.0 delivers phase 1 of milestone M2 (containers): cars, tanks, cargo wagons and rocket silos as chests, and mining drills that drop into them. Containers are in, with a sidecar fix found while checking their exports (#305). What is left: drills at a real base's research, which is far below the harness's and where three gaps show.

The goals keep their order ([GOALS.md](../../GOALS.md)): goal 1, the clock meets its target exactly and recovers; goal 2, the fewest wake-ups. Model corrections are reported apart from search-only changes, and search-only changes keep every result identical. The time spent searching at generation does not grow ([working rules](../working-rules.md), rule 6).

Engine and UI are 0.20.0 and Sidecar the next version (0.12.0 unless the owner sets another), each with one unreleased changelog entry.

## Packages, in order

Packages are named `0.20/<code>`. The numbering follows the board item.

### 1. Containers

**`0.20/containers`** delivers phase 1 of milestone M2: cars, tanks and cargo wagons as chests, holding mixed items in slots that can be filtered, with wagon slots by quality taken from the game data, and a rocket silo as a chest. The silo takes any item into its rocket inventory as far as the rocket lifts its weight, and its rocket part ingredients into its input slots up to the automated insertion limit; with a rocket part recipe of another quality, the ingredients go into the rocket inventory. No craft is run, and an inserter can take from the rocket inventory. Phase 2, a silo that crafts and launches, waits for its launch sequence and timings to be recorded in the game; whether it is then a machine or something new is open. Done when they generate and are checked in the game, then on cells farmed from real bases.

### 2. Drills

**`0.20/drills`**, mining drills at a real base's research. Every drill recorded so far ran at the harness's mining productivity (level 8000), where a drill never limits. Three gaps show only below that. The engine reads the sidecar's exported level as (level − 1) × 10%, while the game appears to give level × 10% for the same number (read from the code, not measured). A drill making under one item a tick mines nothing in the simulator, which rounds its output down every tick; at the owner's base the car build's drill would make 0.77 to 0.93 a tick. And when a full chest gets room, the game's drill refills it over two ticks (5, then 11 items) where the simulator refills 16 in one; end states and hands agree. Record a drill in the game at a low research level set by script, then fix the level reading if the game confirms it, carry a drill's progress between ticks, and model the refill if the recording shows it matters. Done when the low-level recordings match the simulator, and every existing drill job's change is stated.

### 3. Drops the sidecar does not export

**`0.20/left-out`** (#305): Sidecar 0.12.0 lists an inserter whose drop target is a belt or chest not in the export as left out, with its reason, instead of pointing it at belt or chest 1, which made the first silo and wagon exports silently wrong. A unit test checks that the selection tool can select every entity type the extraction reads; its first run found the linked belt missing, which is added. Merged.

## Not in this release

- **Later themed releases:** the other packages first planned for 0.20.0 are in [0.21.0.md](0.21.0.md) (trust the export), [0.22.0.md](0.22.0.md) (faster generation, truer ranking), [0.23.0.md](0.23.0.md) (lanes and recovery) and [0.24.0.md](0.24.0.md) (Gleba). Work with no release of its own is in [later.md](later.md).
- **Containers beyond phase 1:** phase 2 of milestone M2, a rocket silo that crafts and launches ([later.md](later.md)). Moving trains, a wagon swapped at a station, are not modelled ([game-data-and-scope.md](../game-data-and-scope.md)).
- **Follow-ups for later:** follow-up F8, the capture contract, and follow-up F9, the Pickup details UI.
- **Out of scope, as in 0.19.0:** splitters as the pickup tile, and pickups from a side-loaded belt tile. A belt fed through a splitter upstream stays modelled as a plain belt unless a real build shows a wrong verdict. Shared withdrawals upstream, sparse or independently phased supply and general conveyor networks stay unsupported. Also not attempted: live inventory or arm snapshots, simulating fluids, power or circuits in general, and rebuilding beacons.
- **Known limits:** those of 0.19.0 stay as they are unless a package reaches them. The ones the later plans reach: spoilage (`0.24/l2`), the wake-up mechanisms (`0.22/w1`, `0.22/w2`), shared upstream withdrawals and a row of copies (`0.23/lane-supply`, `0.23/copies-lanes`) and belt drops by other inserter kinds (`later/f7`).
