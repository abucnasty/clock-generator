# 0.22.0: real cells hold

This is the plan for release 0.22.0: what it is for, its packages in order and what it leaves for later. It is kept while the release is in flight and dropped when 0.22.0 is released. Live status is on the Real Boy board, https://github.com/users/abucnasty/projects/2, item "0.22.0 · Real cells hold"; this page does not follow every merge. The milestones and follow-ups it names are in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).

## What the release is for

Two real cells are not clocked by the generator's general rules: Stupidfathobbit's cell 02 and Thaeln's cell 09. A third build, the owner's grenade build, comes with a clock made by hand that works in the game and, on the full build, puts the grenades onto the belt just in time: with Thaeln's clock, it is evidence for how a clock can be planned. For cell 02 the generator makes no clock that holds the target, and the clock it selects makes less in the game than the simulator says. For cell 09 every planned clock is unstable, so the generator falls back to a search that takes about ten minutes. 0.22.0 finds out why for each and fixes it by a rule that applies to any build, so that both cells hold in the game with recovery.

It advances milestone M4 (stability on real cells).

The goals keep their order ([GOALS.md](../../GOALS.md)): goal 1, the clock meets its target exactly and recovers; goal 2, the fewest wake-ups. Model corrections are reported apart from search-only changes, and search-only changes keep every result identical. The time spent searching at generation does not grow ([working rules](../working-rules.md), rule 6). A sample is evidence and a benchmark, never the target ([working rules](../working-rules.md), rule 5): each package ends in a rule for any build, not a clock for one.

Engine and UI are 0.22.0, each with one unreleased changelog entry.

## Packages, in order

Packages are named `0.22/<code>`. The numbering follows the board item.

### 1. Stupidfathobbit's cell 02

Cell 02 is `REAL_STUPIDFATHOBBIT_CELL02`, a processing-unit cell from the owner's save (#189), which 0.21.0 makes generate. Its evidence is in 0.21.0: the cell's row in the samples' README (`clock-generator/resources/config-samples/README.md`) and its folder, `clock-generator/resources/config-samples/real/stupidfathobbit/cell02/`, with its config, scaffold and pinned result. Until 0.21.0 is merged they are on the branch `feature/trust-the-export`.

**`0.22/cell02-game-gap`** comes first, since a simulator that calls more than the game can give a wrong verdict, and every package after it relies on the verdicts. The selected clock for cell 02 (1 output swing, backed-up belt) makes 62.0 processing units a second in the game where the simulator says 69.09. Find the cause in the game's recordings before any rule changes, and correct the model. Done when the simulator and the game agree on that clock within the samples' usual margin, and no other sample's verdict moves without a game check.

**`0.22/cell02-holds`**: the cell is pinned unstable. No clock the generator makes holds the target of 80 processing units a second; the best, the selected clock above, makes 69.09 in the simulator, as it is and with "Backed-up belts" on. The owner's scaffold of the build makes 82.86 a second with no clock; the cell in the save made 79.96, its output backing up. Find why no planned clock holds it, and plan one that does by a general rule. Done when a clock holds 80 a second in the simulator and in the game on the scaffold, with recovery, and every other sample's result is unchanged or checked again in the game. It starts after `0.22/cell02-game-gap`, so that the clock is judged by a model the game agrees with.

### 2. Thaeln's cell 09

**`0.22/thaeln-cell09`**: a plan and a clock that support Thaeln's build (`REAL_THAELN_CELL09`) through the generator's general rules. Its coal inserter moves 15/8 hands a cycle, which stretches every planned clock to eight cycles (1,344 ticks). None of the clocks planned for it is stable, so the generator falls back to the observed-windows search, which takes about ten minutes, by far the slowest of any sample. It starts from a clock Thaeln made for this build that works in the game, which the owner gave on 2026-10-23, and turns what its windows do into a planning rule. What the generator does today: all eight standard alternatives are unstable at 480 a second; planning with the coal out of the period (336 ticks) is unstable too; and the observed-windows search on that period ends at "no recovery", at about 1,155 wake-ups a minute. The blast leaves it out (its generation alone takes about 1,000 s) until this package runs. Done when the generator plans a clock for this build by a rule that applies to any inserter with a fractional hand count, without the observed-windows search; the clock holds in the game with recovery, and every other sample's result is unchanged or checked again in the game.

### 3. The owner's grenade build

**`0.22/grenade`**: the owner's grenade build, 240 grenades a second over 7 copies. Each copy has four assembling machine 3s making grenades, a foundry casting iron, and four electric mining drills that drop coal straight into the assemblers; the grenades leave on one turbo belt. The owner gave its export, a scaffold, the clock the owner made for it (which works in the game) and the full build of seven copies with that clock. Add it as a sample with its scaffold; record the owner's clock in the game beside the clock the generator makes (the harness records a clock built by hand since 0.21.0); and turn what the owner's clock does, putting the grenades onto the belt just in time, into a planning rule, with what `0.22/thaeln-cell09` learns. Done when the generator plans a clock for the build by a rule that applies to any build, the clock holds in the game with recovery on one copy and on the full build, and every other sample's result is unchanged or checked again in the game.
